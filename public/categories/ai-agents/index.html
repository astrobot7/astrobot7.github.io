 AI Agents

LLM agents can do a decent first pass at on-call work, but everything they read during an incident is untrusted input. These posts are about giving agents real access to infrastructure while keeping what they can do limited by construction, starting with sandboxed alert triage and remediation on Kubernetes.

Letting an AI Agent Near Production Alerts: Sandboxed Triage and Remediation on Kubernetes

Aswin KM published on 2026-11-15 included in AI Agents

Most of an on-call page is reading. An alert fires, and before anyone can fix anything they open the pod, read its events, pull the previous container’s logs, search traces around the time it started, and work out what changed. That part is mechanical enough that an LLM agent with the right tools does a decent first pass. The reasoning turns out to be the easy part. Deciding what that agent is allowed to touch is the hard one.
