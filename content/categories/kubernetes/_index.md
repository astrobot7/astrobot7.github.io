Managed Kubernetes hides most of the machinery, which is fine until something breaks. These posts go below that layer: bootstrapping a cluster by hand on Vagrant VMs to see how certificates, etcd and pod routing fit together, and running ClickHouse as a replicated cluster with the Altinity operator.
