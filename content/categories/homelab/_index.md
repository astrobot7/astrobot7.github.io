My homelab is a small Docker Swarm of ARM boards and an x86 box across two locations, joined over Tailscale, with Portainer deploying every stack from one Git repo. These posts cover how I keep it running without babysitting it, like pinning images by digest and letting a self-hosted Renovate handle updates.
